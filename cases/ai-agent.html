Finding why carts failed, with an AI agent

A cart-availability incident was putting millions of reais in GMV at risk, and nobody could say why. I built an agent with Claude Code that turned a multi-day investigation into a matter of hours.

Role
Customer Success Manager
When
April 2026
Tools
Claude Code, live logistics APIs
Shared at
Claude Meetup Rio #5

The situation

A consumer brand on the platform was losing sales at checkout. Carts were failing because items showed as unavailable, and the volume was large enough to matter to the business.

❓ Como descrever o cliente sem nome? ("a consumer brand" é o padrão)

The problem

The answer was spread across two places nobody was looking at together: the cart data and the logistics setup. Crossing them by hand meant going through tens of thousands of rows and checking each case against live configuration, which we estimated at about two days.

What I did

  1. Sized the impact first

    Before chasing the cause, I estimated how much GMV was at risk from the failed carts, so the investigation had a business priority and not just a technical one. It came out in the millions of reais.

  2. Built an agent with Claude Code

    The agent read the cart dataset and checked each case against the live logistics APIs.

    ❓ Como o agente funcionava, passo a passo? (os slides devem responder)

  3. Found the root cause

    ❓ Qual foi a causa, em termos neutros, sem atribuir erro à plataforma?

  4. Kept the decision human

    The question I took to the talk was where the human fits in a workflow that already runs most of the analysis on its own.

    ❓ O que você decidiu que o agente não conseguia decidir?

What changed

  • The investigation took hours instead of days.
  • The client and the team had a number for the problem, not just a symptom.
