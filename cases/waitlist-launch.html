Case study · Launch · Fashion retail

A global retailer's first digital waitlist launch

A high-visibility launch for a global fashion retailer. My job was to see the risks before peak traffic, keep escalation one message away, and own everything that came after.

Role
Customer Success Manager, account owner
When
May 2026
Areas
Search, catalog, checkout, marketplace

The situation

A global fashion retailer was running its first digital waitlist launch for a high-demand collection. That means a lot of traffic concentrated in a few minutes, on an account where a bad launch day would be visible to the client's global leadership.

The problem

Launches like this tend to fail exactly at the peak, when there is no time to find the right person. The usual answer is a preventive incident with a war room, which costs the whole team a day.

Launch, end to end

  1. Before launchFlagged the launch internally and pushed for a preventive posture
  2. Launch dayOn the account as the escalation path
  3. PeakSeveral times the normal peak in sales
  4. AfterFollow-through and the quarterly business review

What I did

  1. Flagged the launch early

    I raised the campaign internally ahead of time and pushed for a preventive war-room posture before peak traffic.

    ❓ Como foi a preparação antes do lançamento, na prática?

  2. Became the escalation path

    Leadership decided not to open a preventive incident, because my presence on the account was enough to escalate fast if anything broke.

  3. Was on the account on launch day

    ❓ O que você fez no dia? Qual foi o momento mais difícil?

  4. Owned the follow-through

    After the launch I took the loose ends to resolution: a merchandising ranking issue, product page attributes, a payment workaround and marketplace unblocks.

  5. Told the story back

    I wrote the quarterly business review with the launch results, which were shared with the client's global leadership.

What changed

  • Sales ran several times above the normal peak on day one, and the execution was described as smoother than previous launches.
  • The quarter closed with double-digit growth in revenue and average order value.
