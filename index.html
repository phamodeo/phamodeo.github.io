Selected work

A few cases where the technical answer and the business answer turned out to be the same one.

AI · Incident diagnosis

An AI agent to find why carts were failing

A cart-availability issue was putting millions of reais in GMV at risk, and no one could tell why carts were failing at checkout. I built an agent with Claude Code that cross-referenced a dataset of tens of thousands of rows with live logistics APIs to find the root cause.

A multi-day investigation became a matter of hours. Presented at Claude Meetup Rio #5 (June 2026).

Product · Data

From support tickets to a product map

I turned thousands of support tickets from the largest accounts into a product-vertical map. A handful of verticals concentrated most of the volume, and top-tier accounts opened far more marketplace tickets than average.

I took it to Product leadership, who asked for it to go to Engineering.

Launch · Fashion retail

A global retailer's first digital waitlist launch

Coordinated search, catalog, logistics, and engineering for a global fashion retailer's first digital waitlist launch. Sales ran several times above the normal peak on day one.

I stayed on the account through the quarter, which closed with double-digit growth in revenue and average order value.
